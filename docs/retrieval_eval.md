## Reading these numbers

- **`scheme@k` vs `hit@k` is the diagnostic.** If `scheme@k` is high and
  `hit@k` is low, the right page was retrieved but the answer was split
  across chunks or ranked too low — a chunking problem, not an embedding
  problem. If `scheme@k` is low too, the scheme filter or the query
  itself is at fault.
- **Misses listed below are not automatically bugs.** A gold
  `answer_span` is a verbatim string; if a chunker split that sentence,
  the span genuinely is not in any single chunk, and no retriever can
  find it. That is a gold-set or chunking-boundary artefact.
